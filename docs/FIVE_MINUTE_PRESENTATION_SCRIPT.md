# Cortex — 5-Minute Presentation Script

**Audience:** live demo / pitch  
**Timebox:** ~5 minutes  
**Tone:** clear, confident, conversational — not a feature dump  

---

## Opening (20–25 seconds)

> “Before we start — Cortex is a **large** product. In five minutes we can’t cover every module.  
> Today we’ll walk only the **important parts**: how the business runs day to day, how AI helps, how automation kicks in, and how teams stay secure.  
> One more note: Cortex is **multi-tenant** — each company gets its own isolated workspace, data, team, and settings. What you see here is one tenant demo, not a shared public account.”

---

## 1) Dashboard → Inventory → Reorder (~50 seconds)

**Click:** `/dashboard` → Inventory → Reorder  

> “This is the **home pulse**. Revenue, stock risk, pipeline, AR — one screen so leadership isn’t hunting across tools.  
> From here we drop into **Inventory**, then **Reorder**. Cortex doesn’t just list products — it flags what will run out and pushes you toward the next action: create a purchase order, dismiss, or refresh suggestions.  
> So ops isn’t guessing — it’s reacting to live stock signals.”

**Show briefly:** KPI strip → Reorder alerts / Create PO (don’t deep-dive every SKU).

---

## 2) Finance (~40 seconds)

**Click:** `/finance`  

> “Finance is where money becomes visible. Monthly revenue, cash, AR, AP — the same numbers the rest of the system uses.  
> In Cortex, **revenue means collected payments**, not just invoices sitting unpaid. So when finance says twenty-five thousand MTD, that’s real cash movement — and the dashboard and AI stay aligned to that.  
> Invoice → pay → it shows up. Simple loop.”

**Show briefly:** Monthly revenue / AR cards (skip building a full invoice unless asked).

---

## 3) AI Executive Assistant (~50 seconds)

**Click:** `/assistant`  
**Ask (live):** *“What is this month’s revenue?”* or *“Any low stock I should worry about?”*

> “This is the **executive layer**. You ask in plain English; Cortex pulls **live ERP data** — finance, inventory, CRM — and answers with real numbers, not generic chat.  
> It’s not a separate chatbot bolted on. It’s the same business data you’d see in Finance or Inventory, reachable by conversation.”

**Show:** answer + source / snapshot card if it appears.

---

## 4) Five Agents — important (~55 seconds)

**Click:** `/agents`  

> “Beside the assistant, we have **five specialist agents**:  
> - **Finance** — revenue, AR, P&L  
> - **Sales** — pipeline and deals  
> - **Inventory** — stock and reorder  
> - **Operations** — workflows and ops tasks  
> - **Executive** — cross-module synthesis for leadership  
>  
> Think of the Assistant as the front door, and these agents as department specialists. You can run a task on one agent, watch the queue, and see status without leaving the control center.”

**Show:** one agent card + Task Queue (don’t run a long task unless it’s already fast).

---

## 5) Workflows (~30 seconds)

**Click:** `/workflows`  

> “When a process should repeat — reorder alerts, approvals, notifications — that’s **Workflows**.  
> You design the flow once; Cortex runs it so the team isn’t doing the same manual steps every day. AI answers questions; workflows **execute the playbook**.”

**Show:** list / one template — open builder only if time allows.

---

## 6) Team invite (~25 seconds)

**Click:** Settings → Team  

> “Businesses aren’t solo. Owners invite teammates by email. Invitees join **this tenant only** — again, multi-tenancy: their access stays inside this company workspace.”

**Show:** invite button / pending invite row (no need to send a live invite).

---

## 7) Roles and permissions (~35 seconds)

**Click:** Settings → Roles  

> “Invite alone isn’t enough. **Roles and permissions** decide what each person can see and do — Finance view, Inventory edit, Agents use, and so on.  
> So an ops employee isn’t wandering into billing, and a salesperson isn’t adjusting stock unless you allow it. That’s how Cortex stays safe as the team grows.”

**Show:** one role matrix highlight (Owner vs a limited role).

---

## Conclusion (~30 seconds)

> “So in five minutes:  
> Cortex gives you a **live operations home**, **finance you can trust**, an **AI assistant on real data**, **specialist agents**, **workflows** for repeatable work, and **team + RBAC** so the right people see the right things — all inside a **multi-tenant** company workspace.  
> There’s more — CRM, HR, analytics, knowledge base — but this is the spine. Happy to go deeper wherever you want next.”

## Speaker tips

- Say **“we can’t cover everything”** early — sets expectation and sounds professional.  
- Prefer **one live AI question** over many screens.  
- If something loads slowly, talk over it (“while this refreshes…”) — don’t apologize twice.  
- Multi-tenancy: one sentence only — isolation, not architecture deep-dive.  
- End on **value**, not a feature list: *see → ask → act → control who can do what*.
